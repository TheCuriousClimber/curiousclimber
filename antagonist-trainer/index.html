Antagonist & tendon-adaptation training

Build the tissues your sport under-trains

Pulling and gripping sports — climbing, overhead athletics, high-volume rowing — hammer the prime movers while under-loading the tissues that keep the elbow, wrist and shoulder healthy. Answer a few questions and this builder assembles a 4-week antagonist and tendon block: the right loading protocol (isometric, heavy-slow eccentric, or endurance) for each tissue, progressed week to week with a built-in deload.

The method

Why train the antagonists?

Balance the joint

Strengthening extensors, external rotators and scapular retractors offsets the pull-dominant load of your sport.

Load the tendon

Heavy-slow resistance and isometrics drive collagen remodelling and tendon stiffness — the evidence-based route to durable tissue.

Match the protocol

Isometrics for pain & stiffness, eccentrics/HSR for structural strength, higher reps for postural endurance.

Progress & deload

Volume rises across weeks 1–3, then week 4 deloads while holding intensity so the adaptation consolidates.
