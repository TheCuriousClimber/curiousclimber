Every build leaves the shop meeting three standards

Durable. Functional. Purposeful.

We don't chase numbers for their own sake. A good tune-up makes you harder to break, better at the movement climbing actually demands, and pointed squarely at your next send.

01

Durable

Fingers, elbows and shoulders that take the load without cracking. Protection and tissue tolerance are built into every package — a fast car that's always in the shop wins nothing.

02

Functional

Strength that shows up on the wall, not just in the gym. We train the qualities that transfer — grip, tension, explosive pull, staying power — in the ranges climbing uses.

03

Purposeful

Every session earns its place. You pick packages against your own goals and perceived weak links, so nothing on the plan is there by accident.

Under the hood

What every package is actually tuning

Four performance qualities sit under the bodywork. Here's what each one is, how the shop measures it on the dyno, and exactly why moving the needle earns you sends.

🏁 Horsepower
Power

Instantaneous, explosive output

Peak, < 10 seconds

Movement performance during a dyno, deadpoint or other hard, explosive move lasting under ten seconds.

  • Maximum dyno — leg-jump height
  • Maximum campus — arm-jump height
  • Fastest speed-climb time

The higher you jump and the faster you climb, the easier it is to overcome a single hard move — more chance of sending the crux.

🔧 The Engine
Strength

Force into the wall

Peak tension & torque

Capacity to exert force onto the holds during a lock-off, mantle, drop-knee, heel-hook or any move demanding extreme tension.

  • Grip strength
  • Contact strength
  • Core strength
  • Pull-up 1RM
  • Single-leg squat 1RM

The more force you exert into the wall, the easier you stick hard moves and hold body tension — more chance of staying on.

⛽ The Fuel Tank
Endurance

How long you last

Time to fatigue

The maximum duration you can maintain force and body tension before falling from fatigue.

  • Maximum deadhang time
  • Maximum bent-arm hang time
  • Maximum laps before failure

Hold tension, grip and force longer and you stay on the wall longer — more chance of reaching the chains.

🚀 The Turbo
Power-endurance

Repeatable explosive efforts

Power × stamina

A blend of power, strength and endurance: the maximum number of hard, explosive moves you can perform consecutively before failing.

  • Maximum dyno repeats
  • Maximum campus reps
  • Maximum speed-climb repeats

Jump to max height, stick hard moves and pull explosively again and again — beat multiple cruxes on one route.
