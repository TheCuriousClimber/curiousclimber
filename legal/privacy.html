Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 August 2026·Last updated: 6 August 2026
ℹ️
We keep data collection to a minimum. This policy explains what little we collect, how it is used, and the choices you have.

This Privacy Policy explains how The Kinesiology of Climbing, operated by David McWeeny (“we”, “us”), at https://thecuriousclimber.github.io/curiousclimber, handles information in connection with our website, programs and membership (the “Service”). The person responsible for your data is David McWeeny (The Kinesiology of Climbing), Alberta, Canada, contactable at david.mcweeny@gmail.com.

The short version

We keep data collection minimal. Your workout log and your membership-unlock status are stored only in your own browser (localStorage) — they are not sent to or stored by us. Payments are handled by Gumroad; we never see your full card details. We use the limited information we do receive (like your email) to run the membership and, if you opt in, to email you.

1. Information we collect

a) Information you give us. When you subscribe, our payment provider (Gumroad) collects your payment and contact details and shares limited order information with us — typically your email address, the product/tier purchased, and subscription status. If you sign up for our newsletter or contact us, we receive the details you provide (e.g. email, message).

b) Information stored only in your browser. The workout logger and the license-key access gate save data in your browser’s localStorage — for example the sets you log and which tier you’ve unlocked. This data stays on your device, is not transmitted to us, and is not accessible to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

c) Information collected automatically. Our hosting provider may process technical data such as IP address and basic request logs to deliver and secure the site. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services on this site.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the Service — verify license keys, grant and maintain access to programs, and provide support.
  • To process purchases and manage subscriptions (via Gumroad).
  • To communicate with you about your membership, and to send newsletters only if you opted in (you can unsubscribe any time).
  • To secure, maintain and improve the Service, and to comply with legal obligations.

3. Legal bases (GDPR/UK GDPR)

Where this applies, we process data on the bases of: performance of a contract (providing the membership you bought); consent (newsletter, any analytics cookies); legitimate interests (securing and improving the Service, preventing misuse); and legal obligation (e.g. tax/records). You can withdraw consent at any time.

4. Payments

Payments and subscriptions are processed by Gumroad, which acts as merchant of record and handles your payment-card data under its own privacy policy. We receive order confirmations and limited details, not your full card number. Please review Gumroad’s privacy policy for how it handles your data.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use browser localStorage (not tracking cookies) for core features: the workout log and remembering your unlock status. Gumroad’s checkout and any analytics or embedded media you interact with (e.g. YouTube demo videos opened from a program) may set their own cookies under their policies.

6. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited data only with service providers who help us run the Service, such as: Gumroad (payments/subscriptions) and our website host (GitHub Pages). We do not currently use a third-party email-marketing or analytics provider; if that changes, we will update this policy. We may disclose information where required by law or to protect our rights.

7. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses).

8. Data retention

We keep order and communication records for as long as needed to provide the Service and to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations, then delete or anonymise them. Browser-stored data (your log) is kept until you clear it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to object to processing or withdraw consent. To exercise these, email david.mcweeny@gmail.com. For data stored only in your browser, you can delete it yourself at any time by clearing your site data or using the logger’s “Clear log”. California residents (CCPA/CPRA) and EEA/UK residents (GDPR) have additional rights, including the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Canada, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca); Alberta residents may also contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta (oipc.ab.ca).

10. Children

The Service is not directed to children and is intended for adults (see the Terms). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your own device and license key.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the new version will be posted here with an updated date, and material changes will be highlighted where appropriate.

13. Contact

Questions or requests: david.mcweeny@gmail.com · The Kinesiology of Climbing (David McWeeny), Alberta, Canada.
