Are you ready to become more active?

A 1-minute readiness screening — please complete before starting a program
⚠️
Template. This screening is adapted from the PAR-Q+ (2023) for demonstration. For your live, paying product, use the official validated PAR-Q+ and ePARmed-X+ (see eparmedx.com) and follow its terms of use. This tool does not replace medical advice.

Answer each question honestly. It’s a general guide to whether you can begin gradually on your own, or should check with a doctor or qualified exercise professional first. Your answers stay in your browser — nothing is sent to us.

  1. Has your doctor ever said that you have a heart condition or high blood pressure?

  2. Do you feel pain in your chest at rest, during daily activities, or when you do physical activity?

  3. Do you lose balance because of dizziness, or have you lost consciousness in the last 12 months? (Answer No if dizziness was from over-breathing during vigorous exercise.)

  4. Have you ever been diagnosed with another chronic medical condition (other than heart disease or high blood pressure)?

  5. Are you currently taking prescribed medication(s) for a chronic medical condition?

  6. Do you currently have (or have had in the past 12 months) a bone, joint, or soft-tissue problem that could be made worse by becoming more active? (Answer No if a past problem doesn’t limit you now.)

  7. Has your doctor ever said you should only do medically supervised physical activity?

Please answer every question before checking your result.
