The idea

Physiology first

Real progress comes from applying the same principles coaches use — progressive overload, periodization, and recovery — to your goals. Pick what you want to improve, and train it in the ranges the science supports.

Every plan meets three standards

Durable. Functional. Purposeful.

We don't chase numbers for their own sake. Good training makes you harder to injure, better at the movement life demands, and pointed squarely at your own goals.

01

Durable

Joints, tendons and connective tissue that tolerate load. Injury-prevention and tissue resilience are built into every focus.

02

Functional

Strength that transfers to real life — carrying, lifting, climbing stairs, playing sport — not just numbers in the gym.

03

Purposeful

You choose your focus against your own goals and weak links, so nothing in the plan is there by accident.

Pick your focus

Five training focuses

Choose one or two to prioritise. Each targets a distinct physiological quality, trained in the sets, reps and rest ranges the evidence supports.

⚡
Rate of force

Power Output

Move heavy loads quickly and jump higher. Explosive, low-rep efforts that raise rate of force development — jumps, throws, Olympic-lift variations, fast concentrics.

Benchmark: vertical jump · broad jump · med-ball throw

🏋️
Maximal force

Maximal Strength

Produce more force, period. Heavy, low-rep compound lifting that drives neural recruitment and tissue strength — squat, hinge, press, pull.

Benchmark: estimated 1-rep max on the main lifts

🫁
Energy systems

Conditioning & Endurance

Do more work with less fatigue. Aerobic base plus targeted intervals that develop the energy systems behind everyday stamina and recovery.

Benchmark: work capacity · resting HR · interval times

🔁
Repeatable power

Power-Endurance

Sustain quality effort across repeats. Bridges strength and conditioning — circuits and repeated hard efforts with structured rest to resist fatigue.

Benchmark: reps to fatigue · repeated-effort drop-off

🛡️
Resilience

Mobility & Injury Prevention

The work that keeps you training. Joint mobility, rotator-cuff and tendon health, and balanced antagonist work — the foundation under every other focus.

Benchmark: range-of-motion · single-leg balance

Measure what matters

Benchmark your progress

What gets measured gets managed. Track a small set of key numbers per focus and watch them trend — strength, power, conditioning and mobility — so you always know the plan is working.

Strength & power

Estimated 1-rep max, vertical jump, broad jump.

Conditioning

Work capacity, interval times, resting heart rate.

Mobility & resilience

Range-of-motion checks, single-leg balance, reps to fatigue.
