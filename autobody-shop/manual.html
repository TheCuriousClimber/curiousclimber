How manual works

Three steps, then you're driving

Diagnose yourself

Be honest about your weak links. Falling off single hard moves? Pumping out? Blowing the last crux? That points you to a package.

Add your packages

Pick one or two to prioritise. Chasing everything at once is how engines blow. Tap a package below to drop it on your build sheet.

Run & re-benchmark

Train the ranges the package prescribes, log your benchmark lifts, and re-test every 4–6 weeks. The dashboard tells you if the tune worked.

Four upgrade packages

Each package targets a distinct part of the machine. Tap Add to build sheet to assemble your own self-directed plan — it saves to this browser so you can come back to it.

🏁
Top speed

Horsepower

Instantaneous, explosive output — the surge that wins a single hard move. Trains power: maximum dyno height, campus reach and speed off the ground.

  • Max-effort dynos & deadpoints — < 10 s efforts, full rest
  • Explosive pull-ups & jump training — rate of force development
  • Speed-climb repeats for fast twitch — quality over quantity
Targets: Power
🔧
Engine

Push & Pull

How much weight you can push or pull — the raw torque behind a lock-off, a mantle, a heel hook. Trains strength: grip, contact and maximal force.

  • Heavy pulls & lock-off holds — low reps, near-max load
  • Hangboard & contact-strength work — finger & forearm force
  • Single-leg squats & core bracing — whole-body tension
Targets: Strength
⛽
Fuel tank

Work Capacity

How long you can keep pulling before the tank runs dry. Trains endurance: sustained grip, body tension and resistance to the forearm pump.

  • Long deadhang & bent-arm hangs — time under tension
  • Volume laps on moderate terrain — aerobic base
  • Repeaters & capacity intervals — pump resistance
Targets: Endurance
🛡️
Exterior

Aesthetics & Protection

Keep the bodywork clean and rust-free. Antagonist balance and prehab that protect fingers, elbows and shoulders — the work that keeps every other package on the road.

  • Pushing & antagonist balance — offset all that pulling
  • Rotator-cuff & wrist resilience — joint durability
  • Mobility & tissue conditioning — look & move well
Targets: Durability

    Under each package

    The four performance qualities

    The packages are how you shop; these are the physiological qualities they build. Know what each one does — and how it's measured — so your choices are deliberate, not guesswork.

    🏁

    Power — Horsepower

    < 10 second efforts

    Instantaneous movement performance during a dyno, deadpoint, or other hard, explosive move lasting less than ten seconds. The higher you can jump and the faster you can climb, the easier it is to overcome a single hard, explosive movement.

    Maximum dyno (leg jump height) Maximum campus (arm jump) height Fastest speed-climb time

    The payoff: the more explosive you are, the easier it is to overcome single hard movements — like a crux — that demand instantaneous, explosive motion. More horsepower = a better chance of sending.

    🔧

    Strength — The Engine

    Force & torque production

    Your capacity to exert force into the wall and onto the holds during a lock-off, mantle, drop knee, heel hook, or any move that demands extreme tension and torque.

    Grip strength Contact strength Core strength Pull-up 1RM Single-leg squat 1RM

    The payoff: the more force you can drive into the wall, the easier it is to "stick" hard moves and generate the body tension that keeps you on the rock. A bigger engine = a better chance of sending.

    ⛽

    Endurance — The Fuel Tank

    Sustained tension before fatigue

    The maximum duration you can maintain force in the wall and hold body tension before falling due to fatigue — how big your tank is before it runs dry.

    Maximum deadhang time Maximum bent-arm hang time Maximum laps before failure

    The payoff: the longer you can maintain body tension, grip contact and force into the wall, the longer you stay on the rock. A bigger tank = a better chance of sending.

    🔁

    Power-Endurance — Repeat Runs

    Power + Strength + Endurance

    A blend of power, strength and endurance: the maximum number of times you can consecutively perform hard, explosive movements before falling due to fatigue. It's how you clear crux after crux without fading.

    Maximum dyno repeats Maximum campus reps Maximum speed-climb repeats

    The payoff: the more times you can jump to max height, stick hard moves and explosively pull, the better you overcome multiple cruxes in a single route. More repeat runs = a better chance of sending.
