How the wash works

One session. Everybody runs it.

Like a group class, but themed around the shop. Each day's wash hits a different quality so that over a week the whole vehicle gets cleaned — power, strength, capacity and prehab.

01

Scalable

Every movement has a lighter and heavier setting. Beginners and 8a crushers run the same wash at their own load.

02

Communal

The whole shop runs the same session — post your numbers, chase the board, keep each other honest.

03

Complementary

The wash keeps you moving without derailing a focused tune-up block. General fitness, not a replacement for your build.

The wash rotation

This week's cycle

A sample week. Each wash names its focus so you know which part of the machine you're detailing. Rest days are for the car to dry.

Mon · Power

Quick Rinse

Explosive board dynos & jump work, full rest. In and out — quality over sweat.

Tue · Strength

Pressure Wash

Heavy pulls, hangs and single-leg work. Low reps, big load, long rest.

Wed · Recover

Air Dry

Mobility, easy movement, tissue work. Let the machine settle.

Thu · Capacity

Deep Clean

Laps, repeaters and intervals. Fill the tank and resist the pump.

Fri · Power-End

Full Detail

Repeated hard efforts with structured rest. Clear crux after crux.

Sat/Sun · Send

Take It for a Drive

Go climbing. The wash exists to make this part better.
