Why automatic

You can't read your own tape from the driver's seat

Manual is powerful when you know your weak links. But the fastest gains often come from the things you can't see yourself — a dropped hip, a late clip, an over-gripping hand. That's what a coach's eye is for.

The service process

From tape to tuned in four steps

Submit your tape

Send short clips of you climbing and moving — projects, benchmark boulders, a few basic strength screens. Bring your goals and any injury history.

Video assessment

Your trainer breaks down the footage frame by frame, identifying the physical qualities and movement patterns holding you back.

Lift kit installed

You receive a unique, periodized program — the packages, exercises, loads and progressions chosen specifically for your build and your targets.

Reviews & retune

Check-ins keep the plan honest. As benchmarks move and weak links resolve, the coach re-tunes the kit so progress never stalls.

The personalized lift kit

What the coach dials in

Every automatic build is assembled from the same four packages as manual — Top Speed, Engine, Fuel Tank and Exterior — but the mix, dosage and sequencing are chosen for you.

Diagnosis

Named limiters

The specific qualities and movement faults capping your grade — spotted on video, not guessed.

Prescription

Package mix

How much power vs. strength vs. endurance vs. prehab your build needs right now, and in what order.

Progression

Periodized phases

A scaffolded plan that builds phase on phase, with loads that climb as you adapt.

Goals

Coach-advised targets

Realistic benchmarks and send goals set with your trainer, not pulled from thin air.

Durability

Built-in prehab

Antagonist and tissue work matched to your injury history so the fast car stays out of the shop.

Support

Ongoing reviews

Regular check-ins to retune the kit as your benchmarks and weak links change.
