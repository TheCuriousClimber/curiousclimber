Privacy Policy

Effective: 12 August 2026·Last updated: 12 August 2026
🔒
Short version: the site itself has no accounts and no analytics. The self-guided tools save data only in your browser. Payments are handled by Gumroad, which collects what it needs to process a sale — we never see your full card details.

This Privacy Policy explains how The Autobody Shop (operated by David McWeeny, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use this website and the training content, coaching and memberships offered through it (the “Service”). By using the Service you agree to this Policy and our Terms of Service.

1. Information we collect

a. Data stored only in your browser. Self-guided tools — such as the build sheet — save your selections to your browser’s localStorage. This never leaves your device, is not transmitted to us, and you can clear it any time through your browser settings.

b. Purchase information (via Gumroad). When you buy something, our payment provider Gumroad collects the information needed to process the sale — typically your name, email, billing details and payment method. Gumroad processes this as an independent controller under its own Privacy Policy. We receive limited order details (for example, your email and what you bought) to deliver the product and provide support. We do not receive or store your full payment-card number.

c. Things you send us. If you email us, or submit a video for a coached (“Automatic”) assessment, we receive what you choose to send (for example, your message, email address, and any videos or information you provide) so we can respond and deliver the service.

d. Basic server logs. The site is served as static files (e.g. via GitHub Pages). The host may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and request time) for security and reliability, under its own privacy terms.

2. What we do not collect

We do not run our own analytics or advertising trackers, we do not sell your data, and the Service does not require you to create an account with us.

3. How we use information

We use the limited information we receive to: deliver the programs, coaching or memberships you buy; respond to your enquiries; provide support and handle refunds; and meet legal, tax and accounting obligations. Coaching submissions (such as assessment videos) are used only to prepare and deliver your program.

4. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that make the Service work — principally Gumroad (payments and delivery) and our website host — and where required by law. We do not sell or rent personal information.

5. Cookies

Our pages set no first-party tracking cookies. If Gumroad’s checkout overlay or hosted checkout is used, Gumroad may set its own cookies as part of processing your purchase, governed by its policy.

6. Retention

We keep order and correspondence records only as long as needed to provide the Service and meet legal/tax requirements, then delete or anonymise them. Browser-stored tool data stays until you clear it. Assessment videos are retained only as long as needed to deliver and support your program, and are deleted on request.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Canada’s PIPEDA or the EU/UK GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these, contact us at the address below. For payment data held by Gumroad, contact Gumroad directly. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

8. Children

The Service is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. International transfers

Our providers (such as Gumroad and our host) may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, appropriate safeguards apply.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy; material changes will be posted here with a new effective date.

11. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: david.mcweeny@gmail.com · The Autobody Shop (David McWeeny), Alberta, Canada.
