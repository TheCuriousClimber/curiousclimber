Health & Fitness Disclaimer

Effective: 12 August 2026·Please read before starting any program
⚠️
Read this before you train. The information, programs and tools on this site are educational only. They are not medical advice, physiotherapy, or a substitute for professional care. If you have a health condition, are unsure, or feel unwell, get clearance from a physician before starting.

✕ What this is not

This content is not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, clinical physiotherapy, or in-person personal training. Using it does not create a doctor–patient, physiotherapist–patient, or in-person coach–client relationship.

✓ What this is

General educational fitness and strength-and-conditioning information, including pre-built programs, exercise demonstrations and nutrition guidance, designed for apparently healthy adults who choose to train at their own risk.

Educational fitness tools only

All training content, programs, nutrition plans and interactive tools on this site — including the self-guided build sheet and the readiness screening — are educational fitness tools. They do not constitute clinical physiotherapy, rehabilitation, diagnosis, or medical advice, and are not a substitute for assessment or treatment by a licensed healthcare professional. Coached (“Automatic”) program design is remote strength-and-conditioning coaching, not medical care or physiotherapy.

Consult a professional first

Always consult a physician or qualified healthcare provider before beginning any exercise program, especially if you are pregnant or postpartum, are over 40 and inactive, have (or a family history of) heart disease, high blood pressure, diabetes, joint or bone problems, a chronic condition, or any symptoms of concern. Complete our Physical Activity Readiness screening (PAR-Q) before you start — if it flags anything, get medical clearance first.

Our training content and programs are designed for apparently healthy individuals and are provided in line with the pre-participation screening standards of the Canadian Society for Exercise Physiology (CSEP). If you are not apparently healthy — for example, you have a diagnosed medical condition, symptoms, or are otherwise flagged by the readiness screening — obtain clearance and guidance from a qualified healthcare or exercise professional before beginning.

You assume the risks of exercise

Physical activity — and climbing-specific training such as hangboarding, campusing, dynos and heavy lifting in particular — carries inherent risks, including finger-pulley, tendon, muscle and joint injuries, falls, cardiovascular events, and in rare cases serious injury or death. By using any program or content from The Autobody Shop, you acknowledge these risks and agree that you participate voluntarily and at your own risk, to the fullest extent permitted by law (see the Terms of Service).

Stop and seek help if…

Stop exercising immediately and seek medical attention if you experience chest pain or pressure, pain radiating into the arm or jaw, severe shortness of breath, dizziness, faintness, an irregular or racing heartbeat, sudden severe joint or muscle pain (including a sharp “pop” in a finger), or any symptom that alarms you. In an emergency, call 911 right away. For urgent but non-emergency health questions in Alberta, you can also reach Health Link Alberta at 811. Train within your ability, warm up thoroughly, use appropriate loads, and never push through sharp pain.

Scale to your own level

Programs include easier (regression) and harder (progression) options. Choose the version and load that let you keep good form, and progress gradually. Just because a program lists an exercise does not mean it is right for you on a given day. Finger-intensive training especially should be built up slowly.

Individual results vary

Fitness and climbing outcomes depend on many factors (starting point, effort, consistency, technique, genetics, sleep, nutrition, and more). We make no guarantee of any specific result, and any examples or benchmarks are illustrative, not promises.

Nutrition content

The nutrition plans and any recovery or wellness information are general and educational, and are not a substitute for advice from a registered dietitian, physician, or other qualified professional — particularly if you have a medical condition, allergy, or disordered-eating history.

No professional relationship

Providing this content does not make us your physician, physiotherapist, dietitian, or in-person personal trainer, and does not create any duty of care beyond what is required by law. For individualised in-person guidance, work with an appropriately qualified professional.

By starting a program you confirm

that you have read this disclaimer, are voluntarily assuming the risks of exercise, have completed (or will complete) the readiness screening, and will seek medical clearance where indicated.
