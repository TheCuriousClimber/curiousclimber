The fuel grades

What goes in the tank

Three macronutrients, one job each. Get the balance right and every package runs cleaner.

🥩
The rebuild

Protein

The raw material the shop uses to repair and upgrade tissue after a hard session. Non-negotiable for strength and power gains.

  • Spread across the day — every meal
  • Prioritise after training — recovery window
⚡
The octane

Carbohydrate

High-octane fuel for explosive, high-intensity work. Refills the tank so you can hit power and capacity efforts hard.

  • Load around training — before & after
  • Match intake to volume — more work, more fuel
🫒
The additives

Fats

Steady background fuel and the base for hormones and joint health. Keeps the whole system lubricated and running long.

  • Keep it steady & quality — whole-food sources
  • Don't crowd out training carbs — timing matters
Fill-up timing

When to hit the pump

Before the drive

Top up with carbs and a little protein 1–3 h before hard training so you start with a full tank.

On the road

Long sessions and climbing days? Sip carbs and water through the effort to keep the revs up.

Back in the shop

Protein plus carbs after training gives the shop what it needs to rebuild you before the next session.

Overnight service

Total daily intake and sleep do the heavy lifting. Consistency beats any single perfect meal.
