## Operating rules (apply to every task below)

1. **No paid media.** No boosted posts, no sponsored newsletters, no affiliate payouts. Gumroad Discover is allowed but is optional and takes a revenue share; it is listed as a lever, not a default.
2. **Health first.** Every piece of content passes `tracking/claims-guardrails.md` before it ships. No outcome numbers ("+20% in 4 weeks"), no "cure/fix", no advice that skips the safety gates. Pain or a pop always routes to a clinician.
3. **Community anti-spam.** Links never go in a Reddit post body. The product is mentioned only when a reader asks or when the post is in a thread that permits it. One brand-relevant post per subreddit per week, maximum. Disclose the author relationship every time the guide is named.
4. **Token discipline for Sonnet agents.** Each task carries a word ceiling and a "definition of done". Do not exceed the ceiling; do not rewrite existing pages unless a task says so. Read the sitemap before creating any page to avoid duplicates.
5. **Measure before you optimize.** Nothing on `practice.html` changes until Week 1 instrumentation is live and one week of baseline exists.

## Weekly cadence

- **Monday:** `sales-reporter` appends last week's row to `tracking/kpi-log.csv` and posts a 10-line summary (gross, net, visits by source, top article, top community thread, open issues).
- **Tuesday–Thursday:** content and community execution per `01-30-day-acquisition-plan.md`.
- **Friday:** orchestrator reviews the KPI row against the funnel targets and picks next week's single biggest lever.
