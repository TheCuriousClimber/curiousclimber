Peer-reviewed sport science is powerful — and almost unreadable if you're not an academic. This hub translates the research on exercise physiology, periodization, and the anatomy of sport climbing into language a first-year student can follow, so you can make training decisions with confidence.

Why this hub exists

Better decisions come from understanding, not marketing

Most climbing advice is passed down as gym folklore. When you understand why a training method works — the physiology underneath it — you stop guessing and start progressing. That's the whole point of buying a structured program instead of winging it.
