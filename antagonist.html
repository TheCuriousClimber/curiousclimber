The method

Why train the antagonists?

Balance the joint

Strengthening extensors, external rotators and scapular retractors offsets the pull-dominant load of your sport.

Load the tendon

Heavy-slow resistance and isometrics drive collagen remodelling and tendon stiffness — the evidence-based route to durable tissue.

Match the protocol

Isometrics for pain & stiffness, eccentrics/HSR for structural strength, higher reps for postural endurance.

Progress & deload

Volume rises across weeks 1–3, then week 4 deloads while holding intensity so the adaptation consolidates.
